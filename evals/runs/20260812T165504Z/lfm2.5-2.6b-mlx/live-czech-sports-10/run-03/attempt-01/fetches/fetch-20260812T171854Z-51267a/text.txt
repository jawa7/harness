Lukas Hornicek: Newcastle United sign Czech Republic international goalkeeper on five-year contract from Braga in deal worth £25.7m
Newcastle United complete £25.7m deal to sign Lukas Hornicek from Braga after meeting goalkeeper's release clause; Czech Republic international arrives on five-year deal and is set to start the season as Newcastle's first-choice 'keeper; Hornicek is Newcastle's fifth summer signing
Monday 3 August 2026 09:27, UK
Image:
Lukas Hornicek has joined Newcastle United on a five-year deal
Newcastle United have signed goalkeeper Lukas Hornicek from Braga in a deal worth £25.7m.
The 24-year-old joins on a five-year contract after Newcastle met his release clause with the Portuguese club. He has been brought in to be the first-choice goalkeeper.
The Czech Republic international becomes Newcastle's fifth summer signing following the arrivals of Ewen Jaouen, Bazoumana Toure, Sean Steur and Aladji Bamba.

Newcastle United sporting director Ross Wilson added: "I'm delighted to welcome Lukas to Newcastle United. His progress in recent years has been remarkable and he has firmly established himself as one of Europe's top young goalkeepers.
"We knew there would be no shortage of clubs interested in acquiring Lukas this summer after everything he achieved last season, but it quickly became evident how keen he was to be a part of what we are building here.
"I'm really pleased we've able to conclude the deal swiftly, which has enabled Lukas to join the squad during the opening days of our pre-season training camp in Spain. Being here for the next week will only benefit him as he gets to know his new team-mates and coaching staff."

Newcastle remain braced for Arsenal to make a formal approach for their captain Bruno Guimaraes.
The Gunners have been exploring the conditions of a deal through intermediaries over the past few weeks.
There has still been no club-to-club contact at this stage despite reports to the contrary, which have left Newcastle puzzled.
